FAQ

A few things to know.

How long does my invitation website stay online?

For Signature and Premium, the digital invitation website remains active through the day of your event. After the event, the website is taken offline to help protect your personal event information and privacy.

Do I receive a backup?

Signature and Premium include a digital PDF backup so you can keep a copy of your invitation for your personal records.

Is printing included?

No. Printing is not included in any package. Digital files are delivered so you can print with the provider of your choice.

What counts as a round of changes?

One round means one consolidated list of changes submitted at the same time. Essential includes one round; Signature and Premium include two rounds.

Can Premium Custom be designed from my own idea?

Yes. Premium Custom is our fully personalized option. You can share inspiration, a reference image, a special background, colors or other design ideas and we build the experience around your vision.

What information do I need to provide?

The client provides the event details, photos, text, links and other information needed for the invitation. Boreal is not responsible for errors caused by incorrect information supplied by the client.
